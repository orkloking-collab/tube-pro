## Rules that keep you out of trouble

- **One link per Facebook post** (the landing page). Not the t.me link, not the blogspot link directly.
- **Rotate the opener lines** — identical captions across many posts/groups trigger spam flags.
- **Never post the same link into 10 groups within minutes** — that's the #1 captcha trigger.
- **Never click your own ads** — check earnings in the dashboard instead.
- The landing page must stay reachable — if you take it down, every old post breaks at once.
- Keep `og:title` / `og:description` on the landing page matching the post text so the link preview looks right.
