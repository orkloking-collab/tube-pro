# KOTODAMA (言霊): The Living Script
### Series Bible, Season 1

> **Logline:** In a world where written words come alive, a loud boy with the worst handwriting in his village discovers his "unreadable" scribbles are the First Script. It's the only writing the Blank cannot erase, and the cult that serves the Blank wants it gone.

**Format:** TV anime, 12 × ~23.5 min (standard 30-minute slot). **Genre:** Shōnen action / comedy / heart.
**Tone:** *Naruto*'s underdog fire and *One Piece*'s warmth and comedy, plus a unique "calligraphy-as-magic" hook.
**Language:** English dialogue; all characters are Japanese, in a Japan-inspired world.

---

## 1. Theme
**"A word is a wish you send to the world. Neat or messy, the world hears it if you truly mean it."** (Grandma Chiyo)

- **Perfection vs. sincerity.** Shiori's flawless calligraphy is *readable*, so it can be *erased*. Kakeru's messy strokes mean something no dictionary can define, so they can't be undone.
- **Promises.** In this world a word is literally a promise to reality. Breaking your word has weight.
- **Being remembered.** The Blank erases names and memories. Kakeru wants to be the greatest Brush Master so that his grandmother, a small-town Fudeshi, is remembered.

## 2. World

| Element | Description |
|---|---|
| **Hinomoto (日の本)** | An island nation of mountain villages, castle towns and paper lanterns. |
| **Sumikawa Village (墨川村)** | "Ink River" village. Kakeru's home, famous for its inkstone quarries. |
| **Hitsuzan Academy (筆山学院)** | "Brush Mountain" academy that trains Fudeshi. Its entrance exam is a single living word written on a giant sheet laid across the courtyard. |
| **Spirit ink (霊墨)** | Ink ground from inkstones and mixed with the writer's spirit. Words written with it *live* for a few minutes. |
| **Fudeshi (筆師)** | "Brush Masters." Ranks: *Kakidashi* (beginner) → *Shodan* → *Gyōsho* → *Sōsho* → *Kami-fude* (legendary). |
| **Kami-moji (神文字)** | The **First Script**: words written *before* dictionaries and stroke order existed. They are unreadable and unerasable. Only one person in recorded history could write them: the master who sealed the Blank. |
| **The Blank (白)** | A hunger from beyond the margins of the world. It erases everything it touches: buildings, names, memories. It was sealed twelve years ago with the kanji **封** ("seal"), and that seal is now fading. |
| **Hakushi (白紙)** | "Blank Paper." A cult of "editors" who serve the Blank and believe the world is cluttered with words. Its officers are called *Margins*. |

**Magic rules (kept consistent on screen):**
1. A living word does what its meaning says: 火 burns, 水 flows, 盾 shields, 龍 becomes a dragon.
2. Power comes from *meaning it*, not from beauty. Correct stroke order makes a word stable and fast, but not stronger.
3. Ordinary living words fade after minutes. **Kami-moji do not fade.** (Kakeru's frog is still burning hours later.)
4. Hakushi erasers can delete any word that can be *read*. Kami-moji can't be read, so they can't be erased.

## 4. Season 1 outline (12 episodes)
1. **The Boy Who Couldn't Write** (書けない少年). *This episode; see `EP01_SCRIPT.md`.*
2. **The Brush That Doesn't Write** (書かない筆). Bokusai's absurd training (writing with his feet, painting water on stone). Shiori demands a rematch.
3. **Stroke Order.** Kakeru is let into Hitsuzan "on probation" thanks to Gonda. Kanemaru makes his life hard.
4. **The Kaisho Clan.** Shiori's father arrives. The cost of perfection. She writes with a borrowed brush.
5. **Margins.** The Third Margin attacks the inkstone quarry. Team-up of Kakeru, Shiori and Kanemaru.
6. **Nijimi's Secret.** Why the ink-blot never faded. Chiyo's past as a Fudeshi.
7. **Bokusai's Student.** Flashback: the sealing master as a boy, in a messy-handwriting mirror of Kakeru.
8. **The Festival of a Thousand Lanterns.** Comedy episode with a calligraphy contest, then a twist ending.
9. **Unreadable.** Nashi returns, unmasked. Kakeru learns he can't control Kami-moji yet.
10. **The Seal Is Fading.** The 封 above the capital cracks. The academy mobilizes.
11. **A Blank Page.** The village's memories are erased. Only Kakeru remembers.
12. **Ink It In.** Kakeru writes his first *deliberate* Kami-moji: his grandmother's name, restoring the village's memory. The White King moves.

## 5. Episode 1 synopsis
**Cold open:** The legend of the Blank and the 封 seal. Kakeru declares he'll be the greatest Brush Master. **Part A:** Late for the exam, Kakeru bows to his grandmother's altar, rescues a little girl's kite with a messy 風 that "looks funny but works," and clashes with Kanemaru. At the exam, Shiori's flawless 龍 becomes a dragon. Kakeru's 火 becomes a fire-frog that sets Gonda's hair on fire. **FAIL.** Shiori tells him a shape no one can read is just noise. At the river, a flashback shows how little Kakeru wished for a friend and wrote Nijimi into being. Bokusai notices the frog *is still burning* hours later. **Part B:** Nashi of Hakushi erases the academy gate, defeats Gonda, and breaks Shiori's brush. Kakeru arrives and is erased again and again until, remembering "a word is a wish," he writes something nobody can read. The stroke won't erase. **Doodle Style: Roaring Scribble** cracks Nashi's mask. At dawn Bokusai reveals it was Kami-moji, and that he taught the man who sealed the Blank. Shiori: "This doesn't make us friends." Kakeru: "So we're rivals? Cool!" In the white void, the White King: *"Find the boy. And bring me his name."*
